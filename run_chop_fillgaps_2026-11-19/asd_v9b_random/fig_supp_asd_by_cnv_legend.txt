Supplementary Figure. Height, head circumference and BMI by autism diagnosis within CNV group.
Mean +/- SE of (A) height Z (WHO), (B) head-circumference Z (UK90) and (C) BMI Z (WHO child; adult centile vs pooled non-carrier adults, mapped to Z) in carriers with (blue) versus without (grey) an autism diagnosis, for each CNV group, the pooled overgrowth (16p11.2 deletion + 22q11.2 duplication) and undergrowth (16p11.2 duplication + 22q11.2 deletion) groups, and, as the non-carrier comparison, SSC probands with ASD versus SSC designated siblings. Numbers under the bars are n. Printed above each group: the age- and sex-adjusted ASD effect (Delta, SD; GAM y ~ ASD + s(age) + sex, plus cohort and, for pooled groups, locus) and its p-value (bold: p < 0.05). ASD status: G2MH subject table, SVIP diagnosis summary; SSC probands = ASD. Data as in the main figure (ages 2-65, latest visit with the outcome and BMI, plausibility QC applied). Carriers with ASD are mostly children and non-ASD carriers include adult relatives; the adjustment for age addresses this, and 20_asd_age_confound.R shows the head-circumference effect holds within children. Head circumference, pooled overgrowth CNVs: Delta +0.34, p = 0.067 (n = 215 / 120); pooled undergrowth: +0.12, p = 0.35; non-carriers: +0.14, p = 0.0016. BMI, pooled overgrowth: +0.10, p = 0.55; undergrowth: +0.21, p = 0.054; non-carriers: +0.12, p = 0.018. Full statistics in supp_asd_by_cnv_tests.csv.
Interaction with carrier status (Table S7, columns int_*): the ASD effect in each carrier group was tested against the ASD effect in SSC non-carriers (carrier group pooled with non-carriers; y ~ ASD + locus + ASD x carrier + s(age, by carrier) + sex + cohort). Head circumference: overgrowth CNVs +0.21 (SE 0.16, p = 0.19), 16p11.2 deletion +0.22 (p = 0.25), undergrowth CNVs +0.01 (p = 0.94). BMI: overgrowth -0.04 (p = 0.79), undergrowth +0.05 (p = 0.68). Height: overgrowth +0.26 (p = 0.061), undergrowth +0.08 (p = 0.43). The same interaction with the PGS covariates is in Table S8.
Sensitivity (supp_asd_by_cnv_sensitivity.csv; G2MH carriers and SSC non-carriers with polygenic scores, same individuals in every row): adding childhood obesity, adult BMI, height and autism PGS (each, then all four) to the age- and sex-adjusted model leaves the ASD effects unchanged. Head circumference, overgrowth CNVs: Delta +0.26 (p = 0.3) with age and sex, +0.27 (p = 0.28) with all four PGS (n = 102 / 68); BMI, overgrowth: -0.03 (p = 0.89) vs +0.01 (p = 0.98); BMI, undergrowth: +0.27 (p = 0.16) vs +0.33 (p = 0.067); BMI, non-carriers: +0.14 (p = 0.0071) vs +0.12 (p = 0.019). Sex is a covariate in every model.
