Supplementary Figure. Height, head circumference and BMI by autism diagnosis within CNV group.
Mean +/- SE of (A) height Z (WHO), (B) head-circumference Z (UK90) and (C) BMI Z (WHO child; adult centile vs pooled non-carrier adults, mapped to Z) in carriers with (blue) versus without (grey) an autism diagnosis, for each CNV group, the pooled overgrowth (16p11.2 deletion + 22q11.2 duplication) and undergrowth (16p11.2 duplication + 22q11.2 deletion) groups, and, as the non-carrier comparison, SSC probands with ASD versus SSC designated siblings. Numbers under the bars are n. Printed above each group: the age- and sex-adjusted ASD effect (Delta, SD; GAM y ~ ASD + s(age) + sex, plus cohort and, for pooled groups, locus) and its p-value (bold: p < 0.05). ASD status: G2MH subject table, SVIP diagnosis summary; SSC probands = ASD. Data as in the main figure (ages 2-65, latest visit with the outcome and BMI, plausibility QC applied). Carriers with ASD are mostly children and non-ASD carriers include adult relatives; the adjustment for age addresses this, and 20_asd_age_confound.R shows the head-circumference effect holds within children. Head circumference, pooled overgrowth CNVs: Delta +0.45, p = 0.022 (n = 188 / 96); pooled undergrowth: -0.05, p = 0.8; non-carriers: +0.14, p = 0.0014. BMI, pooled overgrowth: +0.08, p = 0.69; undergrowth: +0.15, p = 0.36; non-carriers: +0.11, p = 0.032. Full statistics in supp_asd_by_cnv_tests.csv.
Interaction with carrier status (Table S7, columns int_*): the ASD effect in each carrier group was tested against the ASD effect in SSC non-carriers (carrier group pooled with non-carriers; y ~ ASD + locus + ASD x carrier + s(age, by carrier) + sex + cohort). Head circumference: overgrowth CNVs +0.35 (SE 0.17, p = 0.046), 16p11.2 deletion +0.27 (p = 0.17), undergrowth CNVs -0.20 (p = 0.25). BMI: overgrowth -0.03 (p = 0.86), undergrowth +0.03 (p = 0.87). Height: overgrowth +0.29 (p = 0.061), undergrowth +0.15 (p = 0.3). The same interaction with the PGS covariates is in Table S8.
Sensitivity (supp_asd_by_cnv_sensitivity.csv; G2MH carriers and SSC non-carriers with polygenic scores, same individuals in every row): adding childhood obesity, adult BMI, height and autism PGS (each, then all four) to the age- and sex-adjusted model leaves the ASD effects unchanged. Head circumference, overgrowth CNVs: Delta +0.33 (p = 0.2) with age and sex, +0.36 (p = 0.16) with all four PGS (n = 101 / 68); BMI, overgrowth: +0.07 (p = 0.78) vs +0.13 (p = 0.58); BMI, undergrowth: +0.32 (p = 0.092) vs +0.35 (p = 0.058); BMI, non-carriers: +0.13 (p = 0.014) vs +0.10 (p = 0.037). Sex is a covariate in every model.
