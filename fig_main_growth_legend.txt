Figure X. Height and head circumference by age and BMI in 16p11.2 and 22q11.2 CNV carriers, and by autism diagnosis in CNV groups with opposing effects on growth.

(A-D) Height and (F-I) head circumference Z-scores by age in carriers of the 16p11.2 BP4-BP5 deletion (A, F) and duplication (B, G) and the 22q11.2 A-D deletion (C, H) and duplication (D, I), stratified by BMI: BMI at or above the 70th centile (red) versus at or below the 30th centile (blue); carriers between the 30th and 70th centiles are not shown. Each point is one individual (most recent visit with the measurement and BMI; ages 2-40 years). Curves are generalized additive model (GAM) fits with 95% confidence bands. Within each panel, "CNV x age x BMI p" tests whether the joint dependence of the outcome on age and BMI differs between carriers and non-carriers (tensor-product GAM interaction with continuous BMI; non-carrier comparison group from the Simons Simplex Collection and Simons VIP, n = 3,124 for height and 3,103 for head circumference), and "high - low BMI offset" is the overall shift of the high-BMI age curve relative to the low-BMI curve among carriers (GAM with a stratum-specific age smooth; SD units). Sample sizes are given in the panel titles.

(E, J) Height (E) and head circumference (J) in carriers with versus without an autism diagnosis, pooled across CNVs with growth-promoting effects ("overgrowth": 16p11.2 deletion and 22q11.2 duplication) and growth-restricting effects ("undergrowth": 16p11.2 duplication and 22q11.2 deletion); one record per individual, ages 2-40 years. Bars are locus-standardized means +/- SE (each locus weighted by the precision of its estimate), so the difference between bars is the pooled within-locus difference and is not influenced by differences in autism prevalence between loci. Delta and p: pooled ASD minus no-ASD difference (z-test); adj.: fixed-effect meta-analysis of per-locus regression estimates adjusted for cohort, adult status, age and age squared. Values in bold reach p < 0.05 after adjustment. Per-locus results are shown in Supplementary Figure Y.

Data: G2MH, Cardiff, Simons VIP (SVIP), Simons Simplex Collection (SSC) and Simons Searchlight (height only). Participants enrolled in both Cardiff and G2MH are counted once. Z-scores below 18 years use growth references (childsds: height WHO, BMI WHO/WHO 2007, head circumference UK90; head circumference in E and J uses all SVIP visits); at 18 years and above, Z-scores are within-sample age- and sex-adjusted values after Box-Cox transformation. Autism diagnosis: G2MH psychiatric assessment (also covering Cardiff participants), SVIP clinical diagnosis, and SSC probands.
