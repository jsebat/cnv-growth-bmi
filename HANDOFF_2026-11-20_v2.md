# BMI modifier paper, v2 draft (2026-11-20)

The paper was restructured around four questions. All v2 work is in `run_v2_2026-11-20/` and scripts `80`-`89`.

1. CNV x BMI effects on height (main Fig. 1)
2. CNV x BMI effects on head-circumference trajectories and autistic traits (Figs. 2 and 3)
3. CNV x BMI x polygenic score on autistic traits (Fig. 4)
4. CNV x BMI x sex, exploratory (Fig. 5)

## Read this first: the control set changed

Until 2026-11-20 the child "non-carrier controls" of the growth figure were the 2,074 SSC probands. The growth table has
height, weight and head circumference for SSC probands and parents, not for siblings, and `17_main_with_ukbb.R` took every
`locus == "No CNV"` row. The manuscript said the controls were siblings and parents.

- `00_control_set.R` (called from `01_build.R`; env `CONTROL_SET`, default `unaffected`) now builds the stated control set:
  SSC designated siblings from the SSC sibling table (visit age reconstructed), SSC parents, Simons VIP non-carriers
  without an autism diagnosis. SSC probands stay in the table under their own label and never enter the controls.
  `CONTROL_SET=paper_2026-11-19` reproduces the old table.
- `82_growth_blocks_v2.R` keeps only the five analysis groups before it sources `17_main_with_ukbb.R`. Do not run 17
  alone on the new table: the flagged probands would enter its genotype-by-band test as an extra group.
- Old versus corrected results: `run_v2_2026-11-20/growth_unaffctl/old_vs_corrected_controls.csv`. With the old controls the
  pipeline reproduces the 2026-11-19 results file for file (`reproduction_check_vs_v9e.csv`).
- `run_v2_2026-11-20/growth/` is the record of the first v2 run with the OLD controls. Use `growth_unaffctl/`.
- Not rerun on the corrected table: `30b_sex_pgs_sensitivity_boxcox.R` (head-circumference rows of Supplementary Tables
  14-16), `21`/`23`-based older outputs at the top level, Supplementary Table workbook.

## Results in one paragraph

The BMI effect on growth differs by genotype at 2-9 years (height P 1.4e-04, head circumference P 9.2e-04) and not later.
16p11.2 duplication: heavier young children are shorter (CNV x BMI -0.41, P 3.4e-05). Head circumference tracks BMI more
strongly in 16p11.2 deletion (+0.13 all ages, P 0.0074) and 22q11.2 duplication (+0.25, P 2.6e-04; rests on the CHOP records:
+0.09, P 0.28 without them). No pooled all-CNV effect. Longitudinal: BMI precedes faster height gain before 8 y (pooled +0.088,
P 3.7e-14) and not after; for head circumference the effect is small and not robust (pooled +0.028, P 0.013; CHOP alone P 0.43).
(2026-11-21: pooled longitudinal models are adjusted for genotype and no longer for cohort, which is confounded with genotype;
per-genotype models keep cohort. Earlier pooled values: +0.089, P 1.9e-14 and +0.030, P 0.0089.)
SRS rises with BMI more steeply in carriers than in relatives (+0.072, P 0.013), concentrated in carriers with a low autism
polygenic score (three-way -0.075, P 0.012, permutation P 0.018; G2MH is 81% of the genotyped carriers). Sex differences and
the BMI-diagnosis association are not robust. Mendelian randomization supports childhood adiposity -> earlier menarche ->
shorter adult height in UK Biobank non-carrier women (partly offsetting the direct positive effect), and is inconclusive for
BMI -> autistic traits in carriers.

## Replication by other sites

`federated/` (see its README and `HANDOFF_Kelly.md`) runs the non-genetic analyses on another site's data and returns
aggregates only, including trajectories as spline coefficients and binned means so that no individual value leaves a site.
