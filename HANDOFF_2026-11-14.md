## Headline (as now written in the manuscript)

1. **Two-way CNV x BMI on autistic traits.** In the combined sample (G2MH + Simons VIP + Simons Searchlight + SSC carriers; 665 carriers
   with SRS, 566 with SCQ; reference SSC unaffected relatives; Box-Cox scale) the BMI slope is steeper in carriers than in relatives:
   SRS +0.087 (P 0.006; ordinal P 0.044), SCQ +0.061 (P 0.077). No heterogeneity between cohorts (P 0.86); present in carriers without an
   autism diagnosis (SRS +0.079, P 0.031). Driven mostly by the 22q11.2 deletion (+0.11, P 0.021; G2MH only); 16p11.2 weak in every cohort.
2. **Sex.** Stronger in female carriers (SRS +0.14, P 0.003) than male (+0.06, P 0.19); difference from separately fitted sexes P 0.029.
   A smaller female > male tilt exists in SSC siblings, probands and parents (none significant), so a carrier-specific sex effect is not
   established. The single-model sex test (P 0.0003) was inflated by shared age / cohort terms and is not used.
3. **Three-way CNV x BMI x autism polygenic score (exploratory).** In genotyped carriers (G2MH, Simons VIP genomes, SSC; n 453) BMI tracks
   SRS only in carriers with a below-median autism score. Primary (sex-adjusted) estimates: within carriers -0.072 (P 0.049); against SSC
   relatives -0.057 (P 0.13). Unadjusted: -0.085 (P 0.015) / -0.072 (P 0.051). Null for SCQ. No BMI x score interaction in SSC relatives,
   SSC autistic probands, or any SSC group (54, 56). Robust within G2MH to scale, ordinal model, covariate x E / x G terms (age, sex, CNV,
   10 PCs), other nine scores, medication, ASD diagnosis, influential points, permutation (P 0.009), site, ascertainment reason (57, 58).
   Simons VIP alone: same direction, not significant (n 91). Needs the Searchlight WGS for replication.
4. **Polygenic scores do not explain the BMI association** (Supplementary Fig. 7): ten scores (adiposity, growth, neurodevelopmental) leave
   the slope unchanged; Mendelian randomization with adiposity scores is weak (F < 10) and, for SRS, inconsistent (Sargan P < 0.001).

## Caveats to keep in the write-up

- SCQ is consistently weaker than SRS (SCQ lifetime scores behaviour at age 4-5; SRS is current).
- Excluding autism-diagnosed carriers truncates the outcome (conservative).
- Within-carrier three-way could in principle arise from clinical ascertainment of carriers; it is as strong in carriers tested for
  non-neurodevelopmental reasons (58), which argues against this.
- Female controls carry higher autism scores (SSC siblings P 0.028, parents P 0.012); carriers do not.
