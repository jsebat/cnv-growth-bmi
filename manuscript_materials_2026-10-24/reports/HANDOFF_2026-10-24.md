## Data notes that must carry forward

* **SSC ages.** The SSC v15 age columns are a September-2012 snapshot, ~3 y older than the visit. Sibling and parent visit age =
  proband "Age at Evaluation" (`ssc_case_HC.csv`) + birth-month difference. Implemented in 21 (`sibage`, `parage`); documented in
  `.../Autism/SSC_Version_15_Phenotype_Data_Set_9/README_ages_and_line_endings.md`. Molly's genomatrix still carries snapshot ages
  for SSC parents; `analysis_table.rds` (adult BMI reference) too.
* **Implausible values.** 106 visit rows / 95 individuals flagged (`implausible_height_weight_corrections.csv`): metre-coded heights
  (G2MH site 213), heights of 366-640 cm (Searchlight), adult BMI 60-181 (SSC parents), a BMI of 9.2 (G2MH release), head
  circumferences outside 30-70 cm or |Z| > 5, children with |height Z| or |BMI Z| > 5. Current handling: metres x100, everything else
  set to missing. **To add records back:** fill `action` and `corrected_*` in the corrections file; `qc_growth()` applies it on the
  next run (genomatrix rows). G2MH-release medical-history rows (used only for CNB BMI) need the same edit in 21 (`relb`) if corrected.
* **Three-way (CNV x age x BMI) terms are fragile.** Removing a handful of extreme heights moved several height three-way p-values by
  orders of magnitude (section 9 of the report). Do not headline them; the CNV x BMI shift tests and SRS/SCQ slopes were stable.
* **No UK Biobank** in the current figures; it can be added back to the height/HC control row (script 17 machinery on Expanse).
* ASD status: G2MH `Jon/Claude/G2MH_CNV_growth/subjects.csv`, SVIP `diagnosis_summary.csv`, SSC probands = ASD. SCQ (and SRS in some
  cohorts) contributed to ASD assignment, so "non-ASD carrier" analyses select on the outcome.

## Headline results (post-QC, provisional)

* Head circumference: 16p11.2 deletion is the only locus whose HC-BMI coupling exceeds unaffected relatives (CNV x BMI +0.13,
  p = 0.021; holds in non-ASD carriers). Height: no constant CNV x BMI shift at any locus.
* SRS and SCQ rise with BMI in carriers (all CNVs +0.21 and +0.34 SD/SD vs controls, p ~ 1e-6 and 5e-5), not in unaffected relatives
  and not in SSC probands with ASD and no CNV; no heterogeneity across the four CNVs; not explained by medication, sex or IQ;
  ASD status attenuates the SRS but not the SCQ slope.
* Penn CNB: no robust BMI effects. The 16p11.2 duplication accuracy slope (-0.14, p = 0.07) and the across-CNV heterogeneity
  (p = 0.11) lost significance after QC removed one implausible BMI; suggestive only.
