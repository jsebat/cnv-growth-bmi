# Figure legends (manuscript materials, 2026-10-24)

## Main figure (fig_main_v3)

Figure. Body mass and growth and social-communication phenotypes in carriers of 16p11.2 and 22q11.2 CNVs versus unaffected relatives.
Columns: height (A-E), head circumference (F-J), SRS (K-O) and SCQ (P-T). Rows: unaffected non-carrier controls (top; SSC designated siblings and parents, plus SVIP non-carrier family members without ASD for height and head circumference), then 16p11.2 deletion, 16p11.2 duplication, 22q11.2 deletion and 22q11.2 duplication carriers (G2MH, SVIP, Searchlight, Cardiff). No UK Biobank data are included.
A-J: height and head circumference Z (WHO/UK90 references) against age, for individuals with BMI at or above the 70th centile (red) or at or below the 30th centile (teal); GAM smooths with 95% confidence bands. Sibling and parent ages are ages at the study visit. n: controls 3533 (height) and 3515 (head circumference); carriers 298/148/268/84 (height) and 180/90/184/67 (head circumference). P-values: CNV x BMI is the age-adjusted difference between carriers and controls in the slope of the outcome on continuous BMI Z (GAM y ~ CNV + s(age, by CNV) + s(age, by BMI) + CNV:BMI); CNV x age x BMI tests whether that difference varies with age (tensor-product interaction). Tests use all individuals with BMI, not only the plotted tails.
K-T: SRS total (Z relative to SSC unaffected relatives) and SCQ lifetime total (Z relative to SSC siblings), adjusted for age within group, against BMI Z. Because the BMI effects on these traits do not vary with age, the trait is plotted against BMI rather than age. Points are coloured by BMI tail as in A-J (grey: 30th-70th centile); black line and band: linear fit with 95% CI; diamonds: mean +/- SE in BMI bins (< -1, -1 to 0, 0 to 1, 1 to 2, > 2 SD); dashed grey line: the fit in unaffected controls. n: controls 5486 (SRS) and 1134 (SCQ); carriers 79/34/208/67 (SRS) and 60/21/170/50 (SCQ). Printed values: the BMI slope (SD of trait per SD of BMI; GAM y ~ s(age) + BMI) and the p-value for the difference of that slope from unaffected controls (model as in A-J). K and P also show SSC probands with ASD and no CNV (black; n = 2065 SRS, 1503 SCQ), whose slope does not differ from controls, so the association is specific to CNV carriers rather than to ASD.
Heterogeneity of the age-adjusted BMI slope across the four CNV groups (within carriers, Wald test of the locus x BMI interaction): Height p = 0.75, Head circumference p = 0.21, SRS p = 0.61, SCQ p = 0.96.
Bold: nominal p < 0.05 for that line. Carriers with and without ASD are combined (ASD status in the carrier samples: see unaffected_controls_tests.csv); ASD-stratified and ASD-adjusted estimates are in unaffected_controls_tests.csv and cognition_bmi_slopes.csv.

## Supplementary figure: prospective growth (fig_supp_prospective)

Supplementary Figure. Prospective, age-varying effect of body mass on subsequent growth in CNV carriers under 18 years.
Change in height Z (WHO; top row) or head-circumference Z (UK90; bottom row) from one visit to the next, per 1 SD of BMI Z at the first visit, plotted as a smooth function of age at that visit (line and 95% CI). Columns: 16p11.2 deletion, 16p11.2 duplication, 22q11.2 deletion, and all carriers pooled (including the 22q11.2 duplication children, who are too few, n <= 12, for a panel of their own). Model: mixed GAM with a child random intercept, adjusted for Z at the visit, interval length, sex, and (pooled) locus and cohort; "age-varying p" tests whether the BMI effect differs from zero at any age; "all ages" is the same model with a single constant BMI coefficient (+/- SE). Grey band: 8-13 years. Height: longitudinal visits of SVIP, Searchlight and Cardiff carriers (cleaned, intervals >= 0.5 y; 116 / 48 / 73 children, 248 pooled). Head circumference: the full SVIP v10 measurement series plus Cardiff, G2MH and SSC carrier visits (91 / 46 / 50 children, 193 pooled). Height, weight and head circumference passed the plausibility QC described in the Methods. Effects, standard errors, t and p for the all-ages coefficient and for fixed age windows are in Supplementary Table S3.

## Supplementary figure: SRS and SCQ against BMI with ASD comparison (fig_cognition_bmi_slopes)

Supplementary Figure. Social-communication scores against body mass in CNV carriers, unaffected relatives and SSC probands with ASD.
SRS total (Z relative to SSC unaffected relatives; left column) and SCQ lifetime total (Z relative to SSC siblings; right column), adjusted for age within group, against BMI Z (WHO child Z; adults, centile versus pooled non-carrier adults mapped to Z). Top row: non-carriers, unaffected SSC siblings and parents (grey) and SSC probands with ASD and no CNV (black); fitted lines with 95% CI and mean +/- SE in BMI bins (< -1, -1 to 0, 0 to 1, 1 to 2, > 2 SD). Rows 2-5: 16p11.2 deletion, 16p11.2 duplication, 22q11.2 deletion and 22q11.2 duplication carriers, points coloured by BMI tail (red >= 70th centile, grey 30th-70th, teal <= 30th), black line and band for the linear fit, dashed grey line for the unaffected fit; the panel prints the within-carrier BMI slope (SD per SD of BMI, GAM y ~ s(age) + BMI) with its p-value, the p-value for the difference of that slope from unaffected relatives, and the slope in carriers without an ASD diagnosis. Bottom row: forest plot of the BMI slope with 95% CI by group (SSC siblings, SSC parents, SSC ASD probands, each CNV and all CNVs), for all carriers (filled circles), non-ASD carriers (open circles) and with ASD diagnosis as a covariate (triangles); asterisks mark slopes that differ from unaffected relatives at p < 0.05. Neither unaffected relatives nor SSC ASD probands show a BMI slope (SRS +0.01 and +0.03; SCQ +0.01 and +0.03 SD per SD), whereas carriers do (all CNVs SRS +0.24, SCQ +0.33), so the association is specific to CNV carriers rather than to ASD. Bold: nominal p < 0.05.

## Supplementary figure: CNB accuracy total (fig_supp_cnb_accuracy_total_bmi)

Supplementary Figure. Body mass and Penn CNB accuracy in CNV carriers.
Penn Computerized Neurocognitive Battery accuracy total (mean z across subtests relative to G2MH non-carriers; at least 5 subtests), adjusted for age within group (age smooth removed), against BMI Z (child WHO; adult centile vs pooled non-carrier adults; BMI within 3 years of the CNB), in carriers of (A) 16p11.2 deletion, (B) 16p11.2 duplication, (C) 22q11.2 deletion and (D) 22q11.2 duplication (G2MH). Points: BMI at or above the 70th centile (red), 30th-70th (grey), at or below the 30th (teal); black line and band: linear fit with 95% CI; diamonds: mean +/- SE in BMI bins (< -1, -1 to 0, 0 to 1, 1 to 2, > 2 SD). Printed: the BMI slope (SD of accuracy per SD of BMI) from a GAM y ~ s(age) + BMI and its p-value (bold: p < 0.05). G2MH non-carriers with CNB have no BMI, so no control panel or carrier-vs-control contrast is shown.
Slopes: 16p11.2 del +0.02 (SE 0.04, p = 0.57, n = 140); 16p11.2 dup -0.14 (SE 0.08, p = 0.074, n = 56); 22q11.2 del -0.02 (SE 0.03, p = 0.53, n = 347); 22q11.2 dup +0.06 (SE 0.05, p = 0.24, n = 142). Pooled with locus as a covariate: all carriers -0.00 (p = 0.85), overgrowth CNVs (16p11.2 del + 22q11.2 dup) +0.04 (p = 0.21), undergrowth CNVs (16p11.2 dup + 22q11.2 del) -0.03 (p = 0.26).
Heterogeneity of the BMI slope across the four CNV groups (Wald test of the locus x BMI interaction in a GAM with a separate age smooth per locus): F = 2.02 on 3 df, p = 0.11; the negative slope in 16p11.2 duplication carriers is suggestive only. Before plausibility QC of height and BMI (00_qc_growth.R) this test gave p = 0.027, driven by one 16p11.2 duplication carrier with a recorded BMI of 9.2 kg/m2; after QC no accuracy subtest or speed measure shows significant heterogeneity (fig_supp_cnb_bmi_legend.txt).

- Table S1: height and head circumference (WHO / UK90 Z), clinical cohorts, ages 2-65, latest visit with the outcome and BMI (plausibility QC applied, 00_qc_growth.R). - BMI slope: within-group age-adjusted BMI effect, SD of trait per SD of BMI (GAM y ~ BMI + s(age) [+ locus]). age x BMI p: tensor-product age x BMI interaction within the group (GAM y ~ s(age) + s(BMI) + ti(age, BMI) [+ locus]).
- CNV main effect: age-adjusted mean difference carriers - reference (SD) from the CNV x BMI model. CNV x BMI: difference between carriers and the reference group in the BMI slope, adjusted for age (bam y ~ [locus +] CNV + s(age, by CNV) + s(age, by BMI) + CNV:BMI). CNV x age x BMI: whether that difference varies with age (bam y ~ [locus +] CNV + s(age) + s(BMI) + s(age, by CNV) + ti(age, BMI) + ti(age, BMI, by CNV); p of the carrier-specific tensor product). The three-way terms are sensitive to extreme observations (see unaffected_controls_report.md section 9).
- Reference-group rows give the within-group BMI slope and age x BMI for that reference group itself.

## Supplementary Table S4 notes

- Table S4: sensitivity of the main-figure BMI effects to sex and polygenic background. The main-figure tests are repeated within strata: sex (male / female; carriers and unaffected controls both stratified); childhood obesity PGS (Bradfield et al. 2019 trans-ancestral GWAS, SBayesRC weights) below / above the median; adult BMI PGS (Yengo et al. 2018, SBayesRC weights) below / above the median. PGS were computed with the same weights on the G2MH WGS cohort (carriers) and on the SSC genotype set (controls), residualised on 10 ancestry principal components and z-scored within cohort; the median split is within cohort. Carriers from SVIP, Searchlight and Cardiff have no PGS in this pipeline and appear only in the sex strata.
- Columns: within-stratum BMI slope (SD of trait per SD of BMI, GAM y ~ BMI + s(age) [+ locus]) and age x BMI p; CNV x BMI and CNV x age x BMI versus the unaffected controls of the same stratum (models as in Tables S1-S2); BMI x stratum p: whether the within-carrier BMI slope differs between the two strata (age-adjusted, locus covariate when pooled); PGS main effect and BMI x PGS (continuous) p: within carriers, GAM y ~ s(age) + BMI * PGS [+ locus]. CNB has no control group with BMI.
