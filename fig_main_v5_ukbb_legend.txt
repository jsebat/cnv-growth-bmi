Figure (v5, with UK Biobank adults). Body mass and growth and social-communication phenotypes in carriers of 16p11.2 and 22q11.2 CNVs versus unaffected relatives.
Columns: height (A-E) with the BMI effect on height by age (A'-E'), head circumference (F-J) with the BMI effect by age (F'-J'), SRS (K-O) and SCQ (P-T). Rows as in v3: unaffected non-carrier controls (top), then carriers of 16p11.2 deletion, 16p11.2 duplication, 22q11.2 deletion and 22q11.2 duplication.
A-E: height Z against age for the clinical cohorts plus UK Biobank adults (ages 40-73, open circles; the control panel shows the clinical non-carriers plus a random 3,000 UK Biobank non-carriers, and the tests use the clinical non-carriers plus a random 50,000). BMI tails as in v3 (>= 70th centile, red; <= 30th centile, teal). The printed CNV x BMI p is from the age-adjusted model y ~ CNV + s(age, by CNV) + s(age, by BMI) + CNV x BMI (continuous BMI, 1 df; 17_main_with_ukbb.R). F-J: head circumference, clinical cohorts only (UK Biobank has no head circumference).
Scaling: children (< 19 y; head circumference < 18 y) are scored on the external growth references (WHO height and BMI, UK90 head circumference). Every adult is scored relative to the non-carrier adults of their own cohort, adjusted for sex, age and age squared with the Box-Cox scaling used for the biobank main effects (one lambda per trait and sex, chosen by profile likelihood on the UK Biobank non-carrier fit and applied in every cohort; residual from the within-cohort fit divided by the cohort non-carrier residual SD; head circumference, which UK Biobank lacks, is left untransformed, lambda 1): the clinical cohorts are pooled, with the SSC parents and Simons VIP non-carrier family members as their reference, and UK Biobank is scored against its own non-carriers. Non-carrier adults therefore have mean 0 and SD 1 in every cohort by construction, and the adult part of each panel reads as carrier minus same-cohort non-carrier at each age. The BMI tails (70th and 30th centiles) are taken on this scale. Lambdas (UK Biobank non-carriers: height 0.65 male / 0.60 female, BMI -0.75 / -1.05) and reference sizes are printed in the run log.
K-T: SRS total (Z relative to SSC unaffected relatives) and SCQ lifetime total (Z relative to SSC siblings), adjusted for age within group, against BMI Z. Because the BMI effects on these traits do not vary with age, the trait is plotted against BMI rather than age. Points are coloured by BMI tail as in A-J (grey: 30th-70th centile); black line and band: linear fit with 95% CI; diamonds: mean +/- SE in BMI bins (< -1, -1 to 0, 0 to 1, 1 to 2, > 2 SD); dashed grey line: the fit in unaffected controls. n: controls 5486 (SRS) and 1134 (SCQ); carriers 79/34/208/67 (SRS) and 60/21/170/50 (SCQ). Printed values: the BMI slope (SD of trait per SD of BMI; GAM y ~ s(age) + BMI) and the p-value for the difference of that slope from unaffected controls (model as in A-J). K and P also show SSC probands with ASD and no CNV (black; n = 2065 SRS, 1503 SCQ), whose slope does not differ from controls, so the association is specific to CNV carriers rather than to ASD.
Heterogeneity of the age-adjusted BMI slope across the four CNV groups (within carriers, Wald test of the locus x BMI interaction): Height p = 0.75, Head circumference p = 0.21, SRS p = 0.61, SCQ p = 0.96.
Bold: nominal p < 0.05 for that line. Carriers with and without ASD are combined (ASD status in the carrier samples: see unaffected_controls_tests.csv); ASD-stratified and ASD-adjusted estimates are in unaffected_controls_tests.csv and cognition_bmi_slopes.csv.
A'-E', F'-J': the effect of BMI on the outcome within age bands (2-9, 10-18, 19-39 and 40-73 years), SD of the outcome per SD of BMI with 95% confidence intervals, in controls (grey) and carriers (blue). For each CNV and band one least-squares model is fitted to carriers and controls together, y ~ CNV x BMI + sex + age: the control bar is the BMI term, the carrier bar the BMI + CNV x BMI terms, and the printed p is the CNV x BMI term (bold if < 0.05), placed below the bars when the carrier slope is negative. The number under each band is the number of carriers (controls in the control row). Bands with fewer than 15 carriers are left empty. These replace the smooth BMI-by-age curves used before 2026-10-27, which were sensitive to the model and smoothing (a joint carrier-control surface without a carrier-specific BMI term pulled carrier curves onto the control curves; 40_test_bmi_curve_models.R). Numbers: main_with_ukbb_bmi_slope_by_band.csv (= Supplementary Table 21).
Statistics behind the growth columns: main_with_ukbb_height_tests.csv, main_with_ukbb_hc_tests.csv, main_with_ukbb_bmi_slope_by_band.csv; behind the SRS/SCQ columns: cognition_bmi_slopes.csv. Script: 35_main_figure_v5_ukbb.R (Expanse), which sources 17_main_with_ukbb.R and loads cog_panels.RData from 35a_save_cog_panels.R.
