Supplementary Figure. Body mass and Penn CNB accuracy in CNV carriers.
Penn Computerized Neurocognitive Battery accuracy total (mean z across subtests relative to G2MH non-carriers; at least 5 subtests), adjusted for age within group (age smooth removed), against BMI Z (child WHO; adult centile vs pooled non-carrier adults; BMI within 3 years of the CNB), in carriers of (A) 16p11.2 deletion, (B) 16p11.2 duplication, (C) 22q11.2 deletion and (D) 22q11.2 duplication (G2MH). Points: BMI at or above the 70th centile (red), 30th-70th (grey), at or below the 30th (teal); black line and band: linear fit with 95% CI; diamonds: mean +/- SE in BMI bins (< -1, -1 to 0, 0 to 1, 1 to 2, > 2 SD). Printed: the BMI slope (SD of accuracy per SD of BMI) from a GAM y ~ s(age) + BMI and its p-value (bold: p < 0.05). G2MH non-carriers with CNB have no BMI, so no control panel or carrier-vs-control contrast is shown.
Slopes: 16p11.2 del +0.02 (SE 0.04, p = 0.57, n = 140); 16p11.2 dup -0.14 (SE 0.08, p = 0.074, n = 56); 22q11.2 del -0.02 (SE 0.03, p = 0.53, n = 347); 22q11.2 dup +0.06 (SE 0.05, p = 0.24, n = 142). Pooled with locus as a covariate: all carriers -0.00 (p = 0.85), overgrowth CNVs (16p11.2 del + 22q11.2 dup) +0.04 (p = 0.21), undergrowth CNVs (16p11.2 dup + 22q11.2 del) -0.03 (p = 0.26).
Heterogeneity of the BMI slope across the four CNV groups (Wald test of the locus x BMI interaction in a GAM with a separate age smooth per locus): F = 2.02 on 3 df, p = 0.11; the negative slope in 16p11.2 duplication carriers is suggestive only. Before plausibility QC of height and BMI (00_qc_growth.R) this test gave p = 0.027, driven by one 16p11.2 duplication carrier with a recorded BMI of 9.2 kg/m2; after QC no accuracy subtest or speed measure shows significant heterogeneity (fig_supp_cnb_bmi_legend.txt).
